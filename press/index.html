Sandcastle Siege

A free 360° turret defense game for iOS and Android. You sit in a turret on top of a sandcastle in the middle of a beach island and hold off a candy-colored toy army coming from every side.

About the game

Wind-up tanks roll in from the sea, toy planes and helicopters fill the sky, paratroopers drop onto the sand and landing craft hit the beach. You aim a full 360 degrees and switch between three weapons: a confetti blaster, a jelly launcher with splash damage and homing fireworks.

  • 7 giant toy bosses, one every 5 waves: a wind-up mega robot, an inflatable battleship that fires cannonball broadsides, a candy blimp, a mecha crab, a gummy kraken that dives under the castle and spits ink on your screen, a candy dragon that sets the castle on fire, and Captain Duck with his bomb ducklings. Later, two bosses attack at once.
  • Upgrade cards after every wave, and ultimates (Candy Airstrike or Freeze Pop) charged by kills.
  • Workshop perks that carry over between runs, daily missions, a login streak and a Toy Album of every enemy you defeat.
