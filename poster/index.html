Sandcastle Siege Poster

Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

Sandcastle Siege Poster is a small internal publishing tool used by the developer of the mobile game Sandcastle Siege. It uploads the game's own promotional videos to the developer's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's TikTok account.

Terms of Service

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Who can use it

Only the developer of Sandcastle Siege uses this tool, with the TikTok account that belongs to the game. No other users can sign up.

2. What it does

After the account owner signs in with TikTok and approves access, the tool uploads video files that the owner selects, with the caption and privacy setting the owner chooses. It never posts without the owner's action or schedule.

3. Content

All videos are original gameplay footage of Sandcastle Siege made by the developer. All posts follow the TikTok Community Guidelines and TikTok Terms of Service.

4. No warranty

The tool is provided as is, for the developer's own use.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Data we receive from TikTok

When the account owner signs in, TikTok gives the tool an access token and basic account information (open ID, display name, avatar) for that one account. The tool uses them only to upload videos and to show which account is connected.

2. Where it is stored

The token is stored only on the developer's own computer. It is not sent to any server other than TikTok's, it is not shared with anyone and it is not sold.

3. Other people's data

The tool does not read, collect or store any data about other TikTok users.

4. Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
