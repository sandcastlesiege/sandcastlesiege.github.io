Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Sandcastle Siege (“the game”, “we”) handles information. We keep it simple: the game does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address or contacts.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, upgrades, scores, settings and similar) is saved only on your device. We do not send it to our own servers. Deleting the game deletes this data.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time. On iOS, ads are only personalised using the advertising identifier if you allow tracking. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Purchases

Optional purchases (such as removing ads) are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details; we only learn whether a purchase was made so we can unlock it.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. Because we do not store personal data on our own servers, most requests concern Google's advertising data, which you can manage through your device settings and Google's tools. You can always contact us for help.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.

7. Contact
